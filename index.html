Our approach

Trust is an architectural choice.

We believe intelligent systems should remain subject to human authority. Rather than treating automation as a black box, we focus on identities, explicit permissions, understandable state, and reproducible outcomes.

Our work distinguishes the independently verified engineering core from features on the roadmap. This includes third-party components, which are credited to their original creators.
